## About This Manual

- **Product name**: _ACME Cloud_ (fictional SaaS product for demonstration)
- **Audience**: end-users and workspace admins
- **Assumptions**: User has signed up, created a workspace, and has browser access

---

## Getting Started

1. **Sign in**
   Go to [https://acmecloud.example](https://acmecloud.example). Enter your email and password.
   ![Screenshot: Sign-in page](images/sign-in-placeholder.png)

2. **Create your first project**
   From the dashboard, click **New Project**. Enter a name and optional description.

3. **Invite teammates**
   Use the **Invite** button in the sidebar to add users by email.

4. **Complete onboarding checklist**
   Follow the guided checklist to configure workspace settings.

> 💡 **Tip:** Bookmark the dashboard page for faster access.

---

## Core Workflows

### Create & Assign Tasks

1. Open your project.
2. Click **+ Task** and provide a title, description, and due date.
3. Assign the task to a teammate.
4. Save.

![Screenshot: New task dialog](images/new-task-placeholder.png)

### Track Progress & Dashboards

- Access dashboards via the top navigation.
- Use filters for **status**, **assignee**, and **priority**.
- Export reports to CSV or PDF for stakeholders.

---

## Tips & Shortcuts

- Press `Ctrl + K` (Windows) / `Cmd + K` (Mac) to open quick search.
- Use **saved views** for recurring reports.
- Bulk-edit tasks by selecting multiple rows in the task table.

## FAQs

**Q: Can I reset my password without contacting support?**
A: Yes. Use the **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in page.

**Q: How do I change my workspace name?**
A: Go to **Settings → Workspace → General**.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Currently no, but the web app is fully responsive.

---

## Glossary

- **Workspace** — A shared environment containing projects and users.
- **Project** — A collection of tasks, milestones, and dashboards.
- **Admin** — A user role with elevated permissions (invite/manage users, configure settings).
