# Datasheets (Portfolio Samples)

- **Audience:** Buyers who shortlist products, and the pre-sales engineers who answer their questions
- **Purpose:** Give a buyer enough on one page to put the product on a shortlist, and give pre-sales the specifications they're asked for first.

A datasheet isn't a manual. It sells by being specific: hardware sizes, supported platforms, and limits stated plainly, because a vague claim is the first thing a technical buyer checks.

## What's Inside

- **[Brightmoor Keep 3.2](brightmoor-keep-datasheet.md)** ([PDF](brightmoor-keep-datasheet.pdf)) — A fictional on-premises AI assistant that answers questions from a company's own documents: buyer requirements mapped to features, sizing for three deployment sizes, security and compliance, and editions

## Highlights

- One page, with the buyer's requirements in the left column and the product's answer in the right
- Sizing tiers a pre-sales engineer can quote from, with the assumption behind them stated
- A compliance claim that says what the product supports and what it doesn't certify
- Built from one Markdown source into HTML and a print-ready PDF
