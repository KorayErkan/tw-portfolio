== Safety Requirements

[WARNING]
====
Maintenance must be performed only by trained personnel. Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) is **mandatory** before opening guards or working near moving parts. Failure to follow these instructions may result in serious injury.
====

=== PPE (Personal Protective Equipment)
* Safety helmet and ANSI Z87.1/EN 166 eye protection
* Cut-resistant gloves appropriate to task
* Hearing protection (≥ 25 dB SNR)
* Steel-toe safety footwear
* Protective clothing free of loose items (no jewelry, secure long hair)

=== Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) — Summary
. Notify affected employees that the machine will be shut down and locked out; stop the machine with a normal cycle stop, then press **E-Stop**.
. Isolate **electrical energy**: set main disconnect to **OFF**; apply personal lock and tag.
. Isolate **pneumatic/hydraulic energy**: close supply valves; bleed off stored pressure; apply locks/tags.
. Isolate **mechanical/kinetic energy**: block or brace moving parts (for example, a vertical axis that can drop under gravity) as required.
. **Discharge stored electrical energy**: wait at least **5 minutes** for the servo-drive DC-bus capacitors to discharge. Verify **< 50 V DC** at the DC-bus test points before touching any drive terminal.
. **Verify zero energy**:
.. With isolation in place, attempt a normal start at the **operator panel** (cycle start, spindle start, axis jog). The machine must not respond.
.. Use appropriate test devices: absence-of-voltage tester on the load side of the disconnect; gauges at **0** pressure.
.. Return all operator controls to **OFF**/neutral.
. Perform maintenance.
. Remove tools/fixtures, reinstall guards, clear work area; confirm all personnel are clear of the machine.
. Notify affected employees that locks are being removed and the machine is about to be re-energized.
. Each person removes their own lock and tag; restore energy in reverse order; perform a controlled test run.

.Energy isolation points, locked out
image::cnc-loto.jpg[Main disconnect locked in the OFF position with a padlock and tag, and a pneumatic ball valve locked closed, width=60%]

. Main electrical disconnect — rotated to *OFF*.
. Personal padlock and blank tag — write your name, date, and reason on the tag.
. Pneumatic supply ball valve — closed, with valve lockout device and padlock.

[WARNING]
====
*Stored electrical energy.* Servo-drive DC-bus capacitors stay charged at hazardous voltage after the main disconnect is OFF. Contact can cause electric shock, burns, or death. Wait at least 5 minutes, then verify < 50 V DC at the DC-bus test points before touching drive terminals.
====

[NOTE]
====
Follow facility-specific procedures in addition to this summary. Reference: OSHA 1910.147 (Control of Hazardous Energy), ISO 16090-1 (Machine tool safety), and applicable local regulations.
====

=== Emergency Shutdown Procedures

==== Electrical Emergency
. **Press E-Stop immediately** — red mushroom button on control panel and pendant.
. **Turn main disconnect to OFF** — located at the electrical cabinet — only if it can be reached safely.
. **Evacuate a 3-meter radius** if sparking, smoke, or unusual odors are present.
. **From a safe location, call** the maintenance supervisor and the facility emergency response team.
. **Fight a small fire only if trained and it is safe to do so**, using a CO~2~ or dry-chemical extinguisher rated for Class C (electrical) fires. Never use water on electrical equipment.
. **Do not attempt to restart** until the hazard is eliminated.

==== Mechanical Emergency (Collision, Jam, Broken Tool)
. **Press E-Stop** — do not attempt to clear obstruction while powered
. **Assess for injury** — call medical emergency if personnel involved
. **Isolate all energy sources** following full LOTO procedure
. **Document damage** with photos before disturbing evidence
. **Contact OEM service** for spindle impact assessment if collision occurred

==== Hydraulic/Coolant Leak

[WARNING]
====
*High-pressure fluid injection hazard.* A pinhole leak in a pressurized hydraulic line can inject fluid through the skin, causing serious injury, tissue death, or amputation. Never check for leaks with your hand or fingers; use a piece of cardboard or wood. If fluid penetrates the skin, seek medical attention immediately, even if the wound looks minor.
====

. **Stop machine immediately** — E-Stop if leak is active/spraying
. **Contain spill** with absorbent materials and drain pans
. **Ventilate area** — hydraulic fluid vapors can be hazardous
. **Check for slip hazards** — barricade wet areas immediately
. **Report to environmental safety** for disposal of contaminated absorbents

== Routine Maintenance Schedule

[IMPORTANT]
====
Intervals assume two-shift operation in clean, indoor conditions. Increase frequency for abrasive materials (cast iron, composites) or heavy coolant carryout. Record each task in the maintenance log.
====

[NOTE]
====
*Coolant concentration* is stated in % (volume). A refractometer reads in °Brix; multiply the reading by the coolant's refractometer factor to get concentration:
concentration (%) = reading (°Brix) × factor.
Example: the fictional IronCool 500 coolant has a factor of 1.0, so a 6–8% target corresponds to a reading of 6–8 °Brix. Use the factor on your coolant's data sheet.
====

=== Service Points

.Spindle lubrication points (weekly)
image::cnc-spindle-lube.jpg[Cut-away spindle head with a grease gun connected to the upper fitting A; front fitting B is also marked, width=60%]

* *A* — upper bearing housing fitting.
* *B* — front bearing housing fitting.

The CNC-5000 spindle is grease-lubricated. Per the OEM lubrication chart, apply 2–3 pumps of NLGI-2 grease per fitting with the spindle stopped and locked out. Do not over-pack: excess grease raises bearing temperature.

.Hydraulic power unit (monthly filter change)
image::cnc-hydraulic-unit.jpg[Hydraulic power unit with five numbered service points, width=60%]

. Return-line filter housing — replace the element monthly.
. Differential-pressure (ΔP) indicator — log the reading before and after the filter change.
. System pressure gauge — verify ≥ 42 bar at idle (see fault E-301).
. Oil-level sight glass — keep level above the 75% mark.
. Reservoir drain valve — use for oil sampling and complete drain-down.

Check the ΔP indicator at every monthly visit; if it shows red between scheduled changes, replace the element early.

== Fault Codes

.Priority scale
[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
|Priority |Meaning
|Critical |Stop the machine; do not restart; escalate to OEM service immediately.
|High |Stop production until the fault is corrected.
|Medium |Correct before the next production cycle or shift.
|Low |Correct at the next scheduled maintenance.
|===

[WARNING]
====
Fault actions that involve drive terminals or connectors require full LOTO first. Wait at least 5 minutes after isolation and verify < 50 V DC at the DC-bus test points before touching drive terminals.
====

=== After-Clear Checklist
. Clear alarms; **home all axes**.
. Run a short warm-up cycle (spindle + axes); observe temperatures and loads.
. Verify part accuracy on a test coupon before resuming production.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Do **not** clear and restart repeatedly without investigation. Recurrent or intermittent faults may indicate wiring damage, failing sensors, or drive issues.
====

=== Escalate to OEM/Service When
* Spindle temperature rises rapidly at idle or faults recur after cooldown.
* Servo faults persist after verifying cables, connectors, and supply voltages.
* Hydraulic pressure fluctuates under steady load or ΔP gauge spikes after filter change.

=== Usage Rules
* Complete entries in **permanent ink**; no erasures. Use single strike-through for corrections and add initials/date.
* Record **machine hours** at the start of each task.
* Note torque values, measurements, and test results (e.g., coolant 7.2% concentration from a 7.2 °Brix reading × factor 1.0; backlash 0.006 mm).
* Attach supporting checklists (E-Stop/door interlock tests) and reference their file IDs in the log.
* After any safety or motion-related maintenance, run a **controlled test program** and record results.

=== Retention
Maintain maintenance logs and checklists for **5 years** (or per facility policy). Provide on request to auditors or OEM service.
