# Appliance and Industrial Manuals (Portfolio Samples)

**Audience:** Consumers, appliance installers, industrial operators, and maintenance personnel
**Purpose:** Demonstrate non-software technical writing capabilities for physical products, emphasizing safety, usability, and compliance requirements across different complexity levels.

## What's Inside

This collection showcases technical writing for physical products targeting both consumer and industrial markets:

### Coffee Machine — Consumer User Manual

[PDF](coffee-machine/user-manual.pdf)

- **Audience:** Home users and consumers
- **Highlights:** Comprehensive safety procedures, brewing recipes for various coffee types, routine maintenance schedules, systematic troubleshooting guide, and complete technical specifications

### Cordless Drill — Consumer User Manual

[Read online](cordless-drill/user-manual.adoc) · [PDF](cordless-drill/user-manual.pdf)

- **Audience:** DIY enthusiasts and home users requiring quick onboarding
- **Highlights:** Detailed safety protocols, labeled component diagrams, battery and bit setup, speed and torque (clutch) operation guidance, battery charging procedures, care and cleaning guidance, troubleshooting charts, technical specifications, and compliance and battery-recycling information

### Kitchen Oven — Installation and User Manual

[Read online](kitchen-oven/user-manual.adoc) · [PDF](kitchen-oven/user-manual.pdf)

- **Audience:** Qualified installers (electrical and gas) for the installation chapter; household users for operation, cleaning, and basic troubleshooting; service technicians for technician-only checks
- **Highlights:** Freestanding range with electric convection oven and four-burner gas hob; "If you smell gas" and tip-over safety messages, US electrical and gas installation procedures, cooking mode explanations with USDA food-safety temperatures, gas hob operation, control panel overview, cleaning procedures, and troubleshooting split into user checks and technician-only procedures

### Industrial Machine — Maintenance Documentation

[Read online](industrial-machine/maintenance-excerpt.adoc) · [PDF](industrial-machine/maintenance-excerpt.pdf)

- **Audience:** Industrial technicians and maintenance specialists
- **Highlights:** Preventive maintenance schedule, lockout/tagout protocol including stored-energy discharge, emergency procedures, fault-code diagnostics with a priority scale, recordkeeping templates, and references to applicable standards
