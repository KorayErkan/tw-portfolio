Filler-breather capRemove procedure

Issue 000-01 · 2026-11-20 · Varnholt VH-15 hydraulic power unit (fictional)

Required conditions

  • The hydraulic power unit is stopped and electrically isolated.

Support equipment

None.

Supplies

NameIdentificationQuantity
Lint-free clothLINT-FREE-CLOTHAs required

Spares

None.

Safety

CAUTION

Do not let dirt into the reservoir. Dirt in the hydraulic oil can cause damage to the pump and the valves.

Procedure

  1. Clean the filler-breather cap and the area around it with a lint-free cloth.

  2. Turn the filler-breather cap counterclockwise and remove it.

  3. Put a clean lint-free cloth over the filler opening to keep out dirt.

Requirements after job completion

  • Install a new filler-breather cap. Refer to Filler-breather cap: Install procedure.
