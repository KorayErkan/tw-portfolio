Level-temperature gaugeRemove procedure

Issue 000-01 · 2026-11-20 · Varnholt VH-15 hydraulic power unit (fictional)

Required conditions

  • The hydraulic power unit is stopped and electrically isolated.
  • The oil level is below the lower mounting bolt of the level gauge. To drain the oil, refer to the oil servicing procedure. Refer to Reservoir: Oil.

Support equipment

NameIdentificationQuantity
Socket wrench, 17 mmWRENCH-171

Supplies

NameIdentificationQuantity
Lint-free clothLINT-FREE-CLOTHAs required

Spares

None.

Safety

WARNING

Hydraulic oil can be hot. Let the unit become cool to less than 40 °C before you start this procedure. Hot oil can cause burns.

WARNING

Make sure that the system pressure is 0 bar. Oil under pressure can cause serious injury.

Procedure

  1. Hold the level-temperature gauge and remove the two hollow bolts.

  2. Remove the gauge and its two seals from the tank wall. Discard the seals.

  3. Clean the sealing faces on the tank wall with a lint-free cloth.

Requirements after job completion

  • Install a new level-temperature gauge. Refer to Level-temperature gauge: Install procedure.
