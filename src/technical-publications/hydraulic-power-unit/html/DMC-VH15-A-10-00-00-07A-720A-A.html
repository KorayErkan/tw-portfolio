Safety

CAUTION

Do not let dirt into the reservoir. Dirt in the hydraulic oil can cause damage to the pump and the valves.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that the part number on the new suction strainer is VH15-1007.

  2. Install the suction strainer on the suction port. Turn it clockwise until it is tight by hand.

  3. Make sure that the inside of the tank is clean and dry.

  4. Put the new gasket and the clean-out cover on the tank.

  5. Install the 12 screws and washers. Tighten the screws to 10 N·m in a diagonal sequence.

  6. Fill the reservoir: Reservoir: Oil.

  7. Examine the clean-out cover for leaks.

Requirements after job completion

  • The suction strainer is installed, and there are no leaks.
