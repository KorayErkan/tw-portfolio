Filler-breather capInstall procedure

Issue 000-01 · 2026-11-20 · Varnholt VH-15 hydraulic power unit (fictional)

Required conditions

  • The used filler-breather cap is removed. Refer to Filler-breather cap: Remove procedure.

Support equipment

None.

Safety

CAUTION

Do not let dirt into the reservoir. Dirt in the hydraulic oil can cause damage to the pump and the valves.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that the part number on the new filler-breather cap is VH15-1004.

  2. Remove the cloth from the filler opening.

  3. Make sure that the filler strainer is in the cap.

  4. Install the filler-breather cap. Turn it clockwise until it is tight by hand. Do not use tools.

Requirements after job completion

  • The filler-breather cap is installed.
