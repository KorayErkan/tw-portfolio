Issue 000-01 · 2026-11-20 · Varnholt VH-15 hydraulic power unit (fictional)

Required conditions

Safety

WARNING

Hydraulic oil can be hot. Let the unit become cool to less than 40 °C before you start this procedure. Hot oil can cause burns.

WARNING

Make sure that the system pressure is 0 bar. Oil under pressure can cause serious injury.

CAUTION

Do not let dirt into the filter housing. Dirt in the hydraulic oil can cause damage to the pump and the valves.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that the part number on the new filter element is VH15-4002.

  2. Apply a thin layer of clean hydraulic oil to the seal of the new filter element.

  3. Put the filter element into the housing. Push the element down until it is fully seated.

  4. Install the filter cover. Turn the cover clockwise until it is tight by hand. Do not use tools.

  5. Remove the lint-free cloths.

  6. Start the hydraulic power unit and let it operate without load for 5 minutes.

  7. Examine the filter cover for leaks. If there is a leak, stop the unit and tighten the cover by hand.

  8. Make sure that the clogging indicator shows green.

  9. Stop the unit. Examine the oil level on the level gauge. If the level is low, add oil: Reservoir: Oil.

Requirements after job completion

  • The return filter is serviceable.
