Level-temperature gaugeInstall procedure

Issue 000-01 · 2026-11-20 · Varnholt VH-15 hydraulic power unit (fictional)

Required conditions

  • The used level-temperature gauge is removed. Refer to Level-temperature gauge: Remove procedure.

NameIdentificationQuantity
Level-temperature gauge, 127 mm, with sealsVH15-10051

Safety

None.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that the part number on the new gauge is VH15-1005.

  2. Put the new seals on the two hollow bolts.

  3. Put the gauge on the tank wall with the thermometer at the bottom. Install the two hollow bolts.

  4. Tighten the hollow bolts to 8 N·m. Do not tighten more: the gauge body can break.

  5. Fill the reservoir to the maximum mark: Reservoir: Oil.

  6. Start the unit and let it operate for 5 minutes. Stop the unit and examine the gauge for leaks.

Requirements after job completion

  • The level-temperature gauge shows the oil level, and there are no leaks.
