Issue 000-01 · 2026-11-20 · Varnholt VH-15 hydraulic power unit (fictional)

General

The VH-15 hydraulic power unit supplies hydraulic oil under pressure to one double-acting actuator. It is a self-contained unit: the reservoir, the motor-pump group, the valves, the filtration and the electrical switches are installed on one frame.

The unit operates with hydraulic oil HLP 46 to DIN 51524-2.

Main data

  • Motor: 5.5 kW, 4-pole, 400 V, 50 Hz, efficiency class IE3.
  • Pump: gear pump, displacement 16 cm³ per revolution, flow approximately 22 L/min.
  • Rated pressure: 140 bar. The pressure relief valve is set to 150 bar.
  • Reservoir: 60 L. The oil volume at the maximum level is 50 L.
  • Filtration: 125 micrometers on the suction side, 10 micrometers on the return side.
  • Control voltage of the directional valve: 24 V DC.
