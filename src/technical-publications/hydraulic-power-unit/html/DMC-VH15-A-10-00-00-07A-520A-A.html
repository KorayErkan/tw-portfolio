Suction strainerRemove procedure

Issue 000-01 · 2026-11-20 · Varnholt VH-15 hydraulic power unit (fictional)

Required conditions

  • The hydraulic power unit is stopped and electrically isolated.
  • The reservoir is drained. Refer to Reservoir: Oil.

Safety

WARNING

Hydraulic oil can be hot. Let the unit become cool to less than 40 °C before you start this procedure. Hot oil can cause burns.

WARNING

Make sure that the system pressure is 0 bar. Oil under pressure can cause serious injury.

CAUTION

Do not let dirt into the reservoir. Dirt in the hydraulic oil can cause damage to the pump and the valves.

Procedure

  1. Remove the 12 screws and washers from the clean-out cover.

  2. Remove the clean-out cover and the gasket. Discard the gasket.

  3. Turn the suction strainer counterclockwise with the strap wrench and remove it from the suction port.

  4. Examine the strainer and the bottom of the tank for dirt and particles. If you find metal particles, stop and tell your supervisor.

  5. Clean the inside of the tank with lint-free cloths.

Requirements after job completion

  • Install a new suction strainer. Refer to Suction strainer: Install procedure.
