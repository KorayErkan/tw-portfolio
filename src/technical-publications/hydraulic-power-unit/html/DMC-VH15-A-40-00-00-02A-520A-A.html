Issue 000-01 · 2026-11-20 · Varnholt VH-15 hydraulic power unit (fictional)

Required conditions

  • The hydraulic power unit is stopped and electrically isolated.

Spares

None.

Safety

WARNING

Hydraulic oil can be hot. Let the unit become cool to less than 40 °C before you start this procedure. Hot oil can cause burns.

WARNING

Make sure that the system pressure is 0 bar. Oil under pressure can cause serious injury.

CAUTION

Do not let dirt into the filter housing. Dirt in the hydraulic oil can cause damage to the pump and the valves.

Procedure

  1. Make sure that the pressure gauge on the manifold shows 0 bar.

  2. Clean the filter cover and the area around it with a lint-free cloth.

  3. Put lint-free cloths around the filter housing to catch drops of oil.

  4. Turn the filter cover counterclockwise and remove it.

  5. Pull the filter element up out of the housing slowly. Let the oil drain from the element into the housing.

  6. Put the filter element in the oil container.

  7. Examine the inner side of the filter housing for metal particles. If you find metal particles, stop and tell your supervisor. Metal particles can show wear in the pump.

  8. Discard the filter element in accordance with local regulations.

Requirements after job completion

  • Install a new filter element. Refer to Return filter element: Install procedure.
