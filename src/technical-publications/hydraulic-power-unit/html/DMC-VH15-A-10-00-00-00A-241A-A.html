Safety

WARNING

Hydraulic oil can be hot. Let the unit become cool to less than 40 °C before you start this procedure. Hot oil can cause burns.

WARNING

Make sure that the system pressure is 0 bar. Oil under pressure can cause serious injury.

CAUTION

Do not let dirt into the reservoir. Dirt in the hydraulic oil can cause damage to the pump and the valves.

Procedure

  1. Drain the oil

    1. Put the oil container below the drain plug.

    2. Remove the drain plug. Let all the oil drain into the container.

    3. Clean the magnet of the drain plug with a lint-free cloth. If you find many metal particles, stop and tell your supervisor.

    4. Install the drain plug. Tighten it to 50 N·m.

  2. Fill the oil

    1. Clean the filler-breather cap and the area around it with a lint-free cloth.

    2. Turn the filler-breather cap counterclockwise and remove it.

    3. Connect the outlet hose of the filter transfer unit to the filler opening.

    4. Fill the reservoir with new oil through the filter transfer unit until the oil level is at the maximum mark on the level gauge.

    5. Disconnect the filter transfer unit. Install the filler-breather cap and turn it clockwise until it is tight by hand.

  3. Bleed and check

    1. Start the unit. Operate the actuator through 5 full strokes to bleed air from the system.

    2. Stop the unit. Examine the oil level on the level gauge. If the level is less than the maximum mark, add oil.

    3. Examine the drain plug for leaks.

Requirements after job completion

  • The oil level is at the maximum mark, and there are no leaks.
