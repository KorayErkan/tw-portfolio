== Safety Information

[WARNING]
====
Improper installation, maintenance, or use may result in fire, electric shock, or serious injury.
Read and follow all instructions before operating this appliance.
====

=== General Safety
* Disconnect from power supply before servicing.
* Keep combustible materials at least 0.5 m from oven surfaces.
* Do not operate with damaged power cord, plug, or control panel.

=== PPE (Personal Protective Equipment)
* Heat-resistant oven mitts
* Eye protection when servicing interior components
* Protective clothing to avoid burns

== Installation

=== Clearances
Provide minimum clearances for ventilation:
* Rear: 100 mm
* Sides: 50 mm
* Top: 200 mm

image::kitchen-oven-clearances.png[Installation clearances, width=60%]

=== Electrical Supply
* 230 V AC, 50 Hz, 15 A dedicated circuit
* Connect to grounded outlet only
* Do not use extension cords

=== Leveling
Adjust four leveling feet until oven is stable and level.

== Operation

=== Control Panel Overview
image::kitchen-oven-controls.png[Control panel with numbered callouts, width=70%]

1. Power button
2. Mode selector (Bake, Broil, Convection, Self-Clean)
3. Temperature dial
4. Digital timer/display
5. Child lock indicator

=== Modes
* **Bake:** 150–250 °C, bottom element
* **Broil:** Top element, 250 °C max
* **Convection:** Fan + heating elements for uniform results
* **Self-Clean:** 3-hour cycle, locks door

[NOTE]
====
Do not attempt to open the door during Self-Clean. Surface temperatures exceed 400 °C.
====

== Cleaning & Maintenance

=== Daily
* Wipe spills with damp cloth after cooling.
* Avoid abrasive cleaners.

=== Monthly
* Inspect and clean door gasket.
* Replace oven light bulb (E14, 25 W).

=== Annual
* Professional inspection recommended for heating elements and control circuits.

image::kitchen-oven-exploded.png[Exploded view with numbered parts, width=70%]

.Callouts
. Heating element — replace if cracked or not heating
. Convection fan — clean blades, check bearings
. Door gasket — replace if torn or hardened
. Light bulb — replace with rated type only

== Troubleshooting

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
|Symptom | Possible Cause | Corrective Action

|Oven not heating
|Tripped circuit breaker
|Reset breaker; ensure dedicated circuit

|Uneven baking
|Blocked convection fan; overloaded tray
|Clean fan; reduce load; rotate trays

|Display shows E1
|Temperature sensor fault
|Check wiring; replace sensor if faulty

|Excessive smoke
|Residue from previous spills
|Run Self-Clean cycle; ventilate kitchen
|===
