This manual explains safe use, setup, operation, maintenance, and troubleshooting for the Cordless Drill (Model **CDR-18V**). Read all instructions before use.

== Safety Information

[WARNING]
====
Power tools can cause serious injury if used improperly. Read and keep this manual. Do not allow children to operate the drill.
====

=== General Safety
* Wear PPE: safety goggles, hearing protection, and work gloves.
* Keep work area clean, dry, and well lit. Do not operate near flammable vapors.
* Maintain stable footing and balance at all times.
* Secure the workpiece (vise or clamps); never hold material in your hand.
* Do not modify the tool. Use only accessories rated for the drill.

=== Electrical & Battery Safety
* Use only charger **CHG-18V** and battery **BAT-18V** (or approved equivalents).
* Do not short-circuit, crush, heat above 60 °C, or incinerate battery packs.
* Charge only indoors, between 5 °C and 35 °C. Unplug the charger when not in use.
* Inspect battery and charger before each use; replace if cracked, deformed, or leaking.

=== Operation Safety
* Remove adjusting keys and wrenches before powering on.
* Select the correct speed/torque and bit for the material before contact.
* Keep hands, hair, and clothing away from rotating parts.
* If the tool smokes, vibrates excessively, or overheats, stop and investigate.

=== After Use
* Turn off and remove the battery before changing bits, adjusting, cleaning, or storage.
* Allow the tool to cool. Store in a dry location out of children’s reach.

== Assembly & Setup

=== Attaching/Removing the Battery
. Slide battery until it clicks.
. To remove, press the latch and pull downward.

=== Inserting Drill/Driver Bits
. Rotate chuck counter-clockwise to open jaws.
. Insert bit; tighten clockwise.
. Verify bit is secured.

== Operating Instructions

=== Bit & Material Selection
[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
|Material | Recommended Bit | Notes
|Softwood | Wood twist, brad-point | Low torque, high speed.
|Hardwood | Brad-point | Pre-drill pilot holes; moderate speed.
|Metal    | HSS twist        | Lubricate; low speed, steady pressure.
|Masonry  | Masonry bit      | Use hammer mode if available; low speed.
|===

=== Speed & Torque
* *Gear 1* → High torque / low speed (screws, large bits).
* *Gear 2* → Low torque / high speed (small holes in wood/soft material).
* Start low; increase torque only if the bit stalls or the screw cams out.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Use the lowest speed that produces a clean cut. Excess speed overheats bits and dulls edges.
====

=== Pilot Holes (Guideline)
[cols="1,1", options="header"]
|===
|Screw Size | Pilot Diameter
|3 mm (≈#4) | 2.0–2.2 mm
|4 mm (≈#8) | 3.0–3.2 mm
|5 mm (≈#10)| 3.5–4.0 mm
|===

=== Technique
. Mark the hole; use a center punch on metal to prevent wandering.
. Hold the drill perpendicular to the surface; brace your wrist.
. Squeeze the trigger gently to start; increase only as needed.
. Ease pressure before breaking through the far side to prevent tear-out.
. For screws: select a driver bit that fully fits the screw head; stop when the clutch clicks.

[WARNING]
====
Kickback risk: if the bit binds, release the trigger immediately and back the bit out. Do not force the tool.
====

== Charging & Maintenance

=== Charging
. Connect charger to mains.
. Insert battery pack; indicator shows charging.
. Charging time: ~60–90 minutes.

=== Storage & Care
* Store between 10–25°C, dry environment.
* Clean dust with dry cloth; do not use solvents.

== Troubleshooting

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
|Problem | Possible Cause | Corrective Action
|Drill does not start | Battery discharged | Recharge or replace battery.
|Chuck slips | Bit not fully seated | Reseat bit and tighten.
|Low power | Incorrect gear/torque | Switch to gear 1, increase torque.
|Overheating | Prolonged heavy load | Rest tool; check ventilation.
|===
