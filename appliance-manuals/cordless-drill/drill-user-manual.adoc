This manual explains safe use, setup, operation, maintenance, and troubleshooting for the Cordless Drill (Model **CDR-18V**). Read all instructions before use.

== Safety Information

[CAUTION]
====
Power tools are dangerous if used improperly. Always read and retain this manual.
====

=== General Safety
* Wear PPE: safety goggles, gloves, and hearing protection.
* Keep work area clean and well lit.
* Do not operate in explosive atmospheres.

=== Electrical & Battery Safety
* Use only the supplied charger (Model **CHG-18V**).
* Do not short-circuit, crush, or incinerate battery packs.
* Charge only between 5°C and 35°C.

== Assembly & Setup

=== Attaching/Removing the Battery
. Slide battery until it clicks.
. To remove, press the latch and pull downward.

=== Inserting Drill/Driver Bits
. Rotate chuck counter-clockwise to open jaws.
. Insert bit; tighten clockwise.
. Verify bit is secured.

== Operating Instructions

=== Power On/Off
* Squeeze trigger to start; release to stop.

=== Speed & Torque
* Use gear selector (1 = high torque / 2 = high speed).
* Adjust torque ring based on material.

[example]
====
Recommended settings:
[cols="1,1,1", options="header"]
|===
|Material | Bit Type | Torque
|Softwood | Wood twist | 8–12
|Hardwood | Wood twist | 12–16
|Masonry  | Masonry bit + hammer mode | 18–20
|===
====

== Charging & Maintenance

=== Charging
. Connect charger to mains.
. Insert battery pack; indicator shows charging.
. Charging time: ~60–90 minutes.

=== Storage & Care
* Store between 10–25°C, dry environment.
* Clean dust with dry cloth; do not use solvents.

== Troubleshooting

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
|Problem | Possible Cause | Corrective Action
|Drill does not start | Battery discharged | Recharge or replace battery.
|Chuck slips | Bit not fully seated | Reseat bit and tighten.
|Low power | Incorrect gear/torque | Switch to gear 1, increase torque.
|Overheating | Prolonged heavy load | Rest tool; check ventilation.
|===
