This manual explains safe use, setup, operation, maintenance, and troubleshooting for the Cordless Drill (Model CDR-18V). Read all instructions before use.

1. Safety Information

Power tools are dangerous if used improperly. Always read and retain this manual.

1.1. General Safety

  • Wear PPE: safety goggles, gloves, and hearing protection.

  • Keep work area clean and well lit.

  • Do not operate in explosive atmospheres.

1.2. Electrical & Battery Safety

  • Use only the supplied charger (Model CHG-18V).

  • Do not short-circuit, crush, or incinerate battery packs.

  • Charge only between 5°C and 35°C.

3. Assembly & Setup

3.1. Attaching/Removing the Battery

  1. Slide battery until it clicks.

  2. To remove, press the latch and pull downward.

3.2. Inserting Drill/Driver Bits

  1. Rotate chuck counter-clockwise to open jaws.

  2. Insert bit; tighten clockwise.

  3. Verify bit is secured.

4. Operating Instructions

4.1. Power On/Off

  • Squeeze trigger to start; release to stop.

4.2. Speed & Torque

  • Use gear selector (1 = high torque / 2 = high speed).

  • Adjust torque ring based on material.

Recommended settings:

5. Charging & Maintenance

5.1. Charging

  1. Connect charger to mains.

  2. Insert battery pack; indicator shows charging.

  3. Charging time: ~60–90 minutes.

5.2. Storage & Care

  • Store between 10–25°C, dry environment.

  • Clean dust with dry cloth; do not use solvents.

6. Troubleshooting

Problem Possible Cause Corrective Action

Drill does not start

Battery discharged

Recharge or replace battery.

Chuck slips

Bit not fully seated

Reseat bit and tighten.

Low power

Incorrect gear/torque

Switch to gear 1, increase torque.

Overheating

Prolonged heavy load

Rest tool; check ventilation.
