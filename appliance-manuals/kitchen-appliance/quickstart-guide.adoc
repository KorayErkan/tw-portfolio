== Unboxing
Verify that all parts are present and undamaged:
* Motor base
* Pitcher with lid (locking)
* Blade assembly (pre-installed)
* Tamper (if included)
* Quick-start guide

[NOTE]
====
If any part is missing or damaged, do not use the appliance. Contact support before first use.
====

== Safety Warnings
[IMPORTANT]
====
Read all safety instructions before use. Keep hands and utensils away from moving blades. Never operate without the lid locked.
====

* Place the base on a flat, dry surface with at least 10 cm of clearance for ventilation.
* Do **not** immerse the motor base in water; unplug before cleaning.
* Keep hair, clothing, jewelry, and utensils away from the blade area.
* Do **not** blend hot liquids with a sealed lid; release steam to avoid pressure buildup.
* Ensure the pitcher is fully seated and the lid locks before pressing *START*.

== Setup
. Rinse the pitcher, lid, and tamper with warm soapy water; dry thoroughly.
. Place the motor base on a flat, dry surface with ~10 cm clearance for ventilation.
. Seat the pitcher on the base until it **locks** (no wobble).
. Insert the lid; press down until the latch clicks. Keep the cap closed unless adding ingredients mid-blend.
. Plug the power cord into a grounded outlet.

[IMPORTANT]
====
The blender will not start unless the pitcher is fully seated and the lid is locked (safety interlock).
====

== First Run (Example Smoothie)
*Ingredients (serves 2):*
- 1 cup cold liquid (water or milk)
- 1 banana (peeled, in chunks)
- 1 cup frozen berries
- 1 cup ice (optional)

.Steps
. Add liquid first, then soft items, then frozen/ice (do not exceed the MAX line).
. Select the *SMOOTHIE* program (or speed 3–4 if using manual speed).
. Press *START*. Blend ~45 seconds (or until desired texture).
. Press *STOP*, remove the lid, and serve.

[TIP]
====
If ingredients stick to the sides, press *STOP* first. Use the tamper through the lid cap while blending, or pulse in short bursts.
====

== First Run (Example Smoothie)
. Add 1 cup liquid + fruit + ice (max fill line).
. Select *SMOOTHIE* program.
. Press *START*. Blend for ~45 seconds.
. Turn *OFF*, remove lid, and serve.

== Controls Overview
image::control-panel.png[Control panel, width=60%]

* *START/STOP* — Begin or end blending.
* *PULSE* — Short bursts for chopping or clearing air pockets.
* *Programs* — Pre-set time/speed profiles:
** *SMOOTHIE* — Soft fruit + liquid; gradual ramp-up.
** *ICE CRUSH* — Short high-power pulses; best for ice/frozen items.
** *SOUP* — Extended blend for smooth, hot-ready textures (use vented lid; do not seal steam).
* *Safety Interlock* — The blender won’t start unless the pitcher is fully seated and the lid is locked.
[TIP]
====
If ingredients ride high on the sides, press *STOP*, reseat the lid, and use *PULSE* or the tamper.
====

== Cleaning & Care
* Unplug the blender before cleaning.
* Rinse pitcher and lid immediately after use to prevent buildup.
* Dishwasher-safe: pitcher and lid (top rack only). Hand-wash blades carefully.
* Wipe the motor base with a damp cloth; do **not** immerse or spray the base.
* Do not use abrasive pads or solvents on any part.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Always remove the pitcher from the base before cleaning. Inspect the lid seal regularly; replace if cracked or deformed.
====

== Quick Troubleshooting
[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
|Symptom | Possible Cause | Fix
|Won’t start | Pitcher/lid not locked (interlock) | Reseat pitcher; lock lid until it clicks.
|Unusual noise | Foreign object or mis-seated lid | Press *STOP*; inspect pitcher, remove obstructions; reseat lid.
|Poor blend / ingredients ride high | Overfilled or program mismatch | Reduce volume; use *SMOOTHIE* for soft items; *ICE CRUSH* for ice/frozen.
|Overheats / smell | Extended high load | Press *STOP*; allow to cool; blend in shorter cycles.
|Leaks at lid | Lid cap or seal not seated | Remove, clean, and reseat cap and seal; replace if worn.
|===
