This quick-start shows the essentials to set up and use your Kitchen Blender (Model **BLN-700**). For full safety and care instructions, see the User Manual.

toc::[]

== Unboxing
Verify that all parts are present and undamaged:
* Motor base
* Pitcher with lid
* Blade assembly (pre-installed)
* Tamper
* Quick-start guide

== Safety Warnings
[IMPORTANT]
====
Read all safety instructions before use. Keep hands and utensils away from moving blades.
====
* Do not operate without the lid locked.
* Do not immerse the motor base in water.

== Setup
. Place the base on a flat, dry surface.
. Seat the pitcher onto the base until it locks.
. Insert the lid and ensure the cap is closed.

== First Run (Example Smoothie)
. Add 1 cup liquid + fruit + ice (max fill line).
. Select *SMOOTHIE* program.
. Press *START*. Blend for ~45 seconds.
. Turn *OFF*, remove lid, and serve.

== Controls Overview
image::control-panel.png[Control panel, width=60%]
* *START/STOP* — Begin/end blending.
* *PULSE* — Short bursts for chopping.
* Programs: *SMOOTHIE*, *ICE CRUSH*, *SOUP*.

== Cleaning & Care
* Unplug before cleaning.
* Rinse pitcher and lid immediately after use.
* Dishwasher-safe: pitcher and lid (top rack only).
* Wipe base with damp cloth; do not submerge.

== Quick Troubleshooting
[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
|Symptom | Possible Cause | Fix
|Won't start | Lid not locked | Lock lid fully; check safety interlock.
|Unusual noise | Foreign object | Stop immediately; inspect pitcher.
|Poor blend | Overfilled or wrong program | Reduce contents; select correct program.
|===
