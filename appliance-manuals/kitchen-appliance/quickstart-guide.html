Unboxing

Verify that all parts are present and undamaged: * Motor base * Pitcher with lid (locking) * Blade assembly (pre-installed) * Tamper (if included) * Quick-start guide

If any part is missing or damaged, do not use the appliance. Contact support before first use.

Safety Warnings

Read all safety instructions before use. Keep hands and utensils away from moving blades. Never operate without the lid locked.

  • Place the base on a flat, dry surface with at least 10 cm of clearance for ventilation.

  • Do not immerse the motor base in water; unplug before cleaning.

  • Keep hair, clothing, jewelry, and utensils away from the blade area.

  • Do not blend hot liquids with a sealed lid; release steam to avoid pressure buildup.

  • Ensure the pitcher is fully seated and the lid locks before pressing START.

Setup

  1. Rinse the pitcher, lid, and tamper with warm soapy water; dry thoroughly.

  2. Place the motor base on a flat, dry surface with ~10 cm clearance for ventilation.

  3. Seat the pitcher on the base until it locks (no wobble).

  4. Insert the lid; press down until the latch clicks. Keep the cap closed unless adding ingredients mid-blend.

  5. Plug the power cord into a grounded outlet.

The blender will not start unless the pitcher is fully seated and the lid is locked (safety interlock).

First Run (Example Smoothie)

Ingredients (serves 2): - 1 cup cold liquid (water or milk) - 1 banana (peeled, in chunks) - 1 cup frozen berries - 1 cup ice (optional)

Steps

  1. Add liquid first, then soft items, then frozen/ice (do not exceed the MAX line).

  2. Select the SMOOTHIE program (or speed 3–4 if using manual speed).

  3. Press START. Blend ~45 seconds (or until desired texture).

  4. Press STOP, remove the lid, and serve.

If ingredients stick to the sides, press STOP first. Use the tamper through the lid cap while blending, or pulse in short bursts.

First Run (Example Smoothie)

  1. Add 1 cup liquid + fruit + ice (max fill line).

  2. Select SMOOTHIE program.

  3. Press START. Blend for ~45 seconds.

  4. Turn OFF, remove lid, and serve.

Controls Overview

  • START/STOP — Begin or end blending.

  • PULSE — Short bursts for chopping or clearing air pockets.

  • Programs — Pre-set time/speed profiles:

    • SMOOTHIE — Soft fruit + liquid; gradual ramp-up.

    • ICE CRUSH — Short high-power pulses; best for ice/frozen items.

    • SOUP — Extended blend for smooth, hot-ready textures (use vented lid; do not seal steam).

  • Safety Interlock — The blender won’t start unless the pitcher is fully seated and the lid is locked.

If ingredients ride high on the sides, press STOP, reseat the lid, and use PULSE or the tamper.

Cleaning & Care

  • Unplug before cleaning.

  • Rinse pitcher and lid immediately after use.

  • Dishwasher-safe: pitcher and lid (top rack only).

  • Wipe base with damp cloth; do not submerge.

Quick Troubleshooting

Symptom Possible Cause Fix

Won’t start

Lid not locked

Lock lid fully; check safety interlock.

Unusual noise

Foreign object

Stop immediately; inspect pitcher.

Poor blend

Overfilled or wrong program

Reduce contents; select correct program.
