This excerpt illustrates routine maintenance, safety procedures, and fault code handling for a fictional CNC machining center (Model **CNC-5000**). Prepared as a portfolio sample.

== Introduction
*Intended audience:* certified maintenance technicians and supervisors.
*Scope:* routine service, selected fault codes, and recordkeeping templates.

== Safety Requirements

[WARNING]
====
Perform lockout/tagout (LOTO) before any service. Failure to comply may result in serious injury.
====

=== PPE
* Safety helmet, goggles, cut-resistant gloves.
* Hearing protection (≥ 25 dB SNR).
* Steel-toe boots.

=== Lockout/Tagout Procedure (Summary)
. Notify operators and shut down machine.
. Isolate power (main disconnect).
. Lock and tag the disconnect; verify zero energy.
. Attempt restart to confirm isolation.
. Perform maintenance; remove locks/tags only after verification.

== Routine Maintenance Schedule

[cols="2,1,1,2", options="header"]
|===
|Task | Frequency | Tools | Notes
|Inspect way covers | Daily | Visual | Replace if torn or jammed.
|Clean chip conveyors | Daily | Scraper/brush | Power off first.
|Lubricate spindle bearings | Weekly | Grease gun (NLGI-2) | 2–3 pumps per fitting.
|Check coolant concentration | Weekly | Refractometer | Maintain 6–8%.
|Replace hydraulic filter | Monthly | Wrench set | Depressurize system.
|Inspect belts | Quarterly | Tension gauge | Replace if frayed.
|===

== Fault Codes

[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
|Code | Meaning | Corrective Action
|E-101 | Axis overtravel | Home the machine; check limit switches.
|E-102 | Spindle overheating | Shut down; verify coolant flow and fan operation.
|E-204 | Servo drive fault | Inspect drive LEDs; reseat connectors; consult drive manual.
|===
