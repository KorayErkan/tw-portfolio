## Document Categories by Value Tier

### Top Tier (Excellent Value)

**SOPs** - Manufacturing, healthcare, and financial services pay premium rates ($50-100+/hour) for compliance-critical procedures.

**Risk Assessments/Mitigation Plans** - High demand across industries, especially with regulatory requirements. Financial services, healthcare, and government contracting value these heavily.

**Compliance Reports** - Strong alignment with regulatory focus. Industries like pharmaceuticals, banking, and aerospace need these regularly.

### Second Tier (Good Value)

**Technical Specifications** - Can be valuable, but often requires deep domain expertise. Consider non-software specs (medical devices, manufacturing equipment) to avoid technical gatekeeping.

**Training Manuals** - Solid potential, especially for compliance training in regulated industries.

### Lower Priority

**SOWs** - Often handled internally or by consultants who also do the work. Less standalone value.

**eLearning Scripts** - Competitive market with specialized tools/platforms.

## Specific Examples for Top Tier Categories

### SOPs (High-Value Examples)
- **Pharmaceutical Manufacturing SOP** - "Sterile Compounding Procedures for Injectable Medications" (FDA-regulated, patient safety critical)
- **Healthcare SOP** - "Patient Data Privacy Compliance Procedures" (HIPAA compliance, applies to all healthcare facilities)
- **Financial Services SOP** - "Wire Transfer Verification and Anti-Money Laundering Procedures" (Banking regulatory compliance)

### Risk Assessments/Mitigation Plans
- **Healthcare Risk Assessment** - "Medical Device Cybersecurity Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan" (FDA requirements, high liability)
- **Manufacturing Risk Plan** - "Chemical Handling and Spill Response Risk Assessment" (OSHA compliance, environmental safety)
- **Government Contractor Risk Assessment** - "Data Security Risk Assessment for Federal Contract Work" (NIST compliance, security clearance relevant)

### Compliance Reports
- **FDA Compliance Report** - "Annual Medical Device Adverse Event Reporting Summary" (Required regulatory submission)
- **Environmental Compliance Report** - "Quarterly Air Quality Monitoring Compliance Report" (EPA requirements)
- **Financial Compliance Report** - "Annual Community Reinvestment Act Performance Assessment" (Banking regulatory requirement)

## Notes
These examples target regulated industries with mandatory documentation requirements, ensuring consistent demand and premium pricing.
