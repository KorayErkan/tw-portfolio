1. Executive Summary

This Quality Assurance Surveillance Plan (QASP) establishes comprehensive monitoring and evaluation procedures for janitorial services at federal facilities. The plan implements a risk-based surveillance approach that ensures consistent service delivery while maintaining compliance with federal health, safety, and environmental standards.

1.1 Key Performance Indicators

  • Service Reliability: 99.5 percent task completion rate
  • Quality Standards: 95 percent customer satisfaction rating
  • Compliance: Zero critical health/safety violations
  • Response Time: 100 percent emergency response within 2 hours

2. Contract Overview

2.1 Contract Risk Assessment

Winter Weather Impacts High Medium Enhanced snow removal protocols and flexible scheduling
Equipment Failure Low High Preventive maintenance program and rapid replacement SLA
Pandemic Response Low High Flexible protocols per CDC guidelines and enhanced PPE

The contractor provides comprehensive janitorial services including:

Sanitation Services Monthly Full OSHA/CDC compliance
Emergency Response As needed Response within 2 hours

2.3 Applicable Regulations and Standards

  • Primary: OSHA Cleaning Standards (29 CFR 1910)
  • Secondary: CDC Guidelines for Cleaning and Disinfection
  • Facility-Specific: Agency Facility Maintenance Handbook
  • Environmental: EPA Green Cleaning Requirements (Executive Order 13693)
  • Emergency: Pandemic Response Protocols (CDC/OSHA)
  • Federal Acquisition: FAR 46.4 (Government Contract Quality Assurance)
  • Performance-Based: FAR 37.6 (Performance-Based Acquisition)

2.4 Sustainability and Green Cleaning Requirements

2.4.1 Environmental Performance Standards

  • Green Products: Minimum 75 percent EPA Safer Choice or equivalent certified products
  • Microfiber Cloth Reuse: Target 50 wash cycles per cloth before replacement
  • Water Conservation: Low-flow equipment and concentrated cleaning solutions
  • Waste Reduction: Recycling program for containers and packaging materials
  • Energy Efficiency: ENERGY STAR certified equipment where available

2.4.2 Sustainability Reporting

  • Monthly Metrics: Green product usage percentage, water consumption, waste diversion rates
  • Quarterly Assessment: Environmental impact analysis and improvement recommendations
  • Innovation Incentive: Bonus consideration for sustainable practice innovations

3. Organizational Structure and Responsibilities

  • Interface with contracting officer for significant performance issues

Primary Responsibilities:

5. Surveillance Methodology

5.1 Risk-Based Surveillance Framework

5.1.1 High-Risk Areas (Daily Monitoring)

  • Restrooms and break rooms
  • Entry points and high-traffic corridors
  • Food service areas
  • Areas with specialized equipment

5.1.2 Medium-Risk Areas (Weekly Monitoring)

  • Individual offices and workstations
  • Conference rooms and meeting spaces
  • Storage areas and utility rooms

5.1.3 Low-Risk Areas (Monthly Monitoring)

  • Archive and document storage
  • Mechanical rooms (contractor access)
  • Seasonal or occasional-use spaces

5.2 Surveillance Activities

5.2.1 Scheduled Inspections

Weekly Walkthroughs

  • Participants: COR, Contractor Project Manager
  • Duration: 60-90 minutes
  • Documentation: Standardized inspection checklist
  • Follow-up: 24-hour corrective action window for minor issues

Monthly Deep Inspections

  • Participants: COR, Alternate COR, Facilities Manager
  • Duration: 2-3 hours
  • Focus: Deep cleaning verification, compliance validation
  • Documentation: Comprehensive inspection report with photographic evidence

Quarterly Performance Reviews

  • Participants: All stakeholders including Contracting Officer
  • Duration: Half-day meeting
  • Scope: Comprehensive performance analysis and planning
  • Outcomes: Performance rating, improvement plans, contract modifications if needed

5.2.2 Unscheduled Surveillance

Random Spot Checks

  • Frequency: Minimum 8 per month
  • Timing: Unannounced, varied hours
  • Focus: High-risk areas and complaint-driven locations
  • Documentation: Spot-check log with immediate feedback

Triggered Inspections

  • Activation: Customer complaints, incident reports, or performance alerts
  • Timeline: Within 24 hours of trigger event
  • Scope: Focused investigation with root cause analysis
  • Follow-up: Mandatory corrective action plan

5.3 Customer Feedback Integration

5.3.1 Feedback Collection

  • Method: Monthly electronic survey (15 questions, 5-minute completion)
  • Distribution: All building occupants via facility email system
  • Response Target: 70 percent participation rate
  • Analysis: Statistical trending with sentiment analysis

5.3.2 Complaint Management

  • Reporting: 24/7 online portal with automated acknowledgment
  • Response: COR notification within 2 hours for critical issues
  • Investigation: 48-hour resolution timeline with status updates
  • Documentation: Complete audit trail in contract management system

7. Corrective Action Procedures

7.1 Deficiency Classification System

7.1.1 Category I - Minor Deficiencies

Definition: Issues that do not impact health, safety, or mission operations
Examples: Single missed trash collection, minor supply shortage
Response Time: 24-hour correction
Documentation: Incident log entry, no formal notice required
Escalation: Three Category I issues in 30 days = Category II

7.1.2 Category II - Significant Deficiencies

Definition: Issues affecting cleanliness standards or customer satisfaction
Examples: Incomplete cleaning, supply stockouts, delayed deliverables
Response Time: 48-hour correction with improvement plan
Documentation: Formal deficiency notice, corrective action plan required
Financial Impact: 2-5 percent weekly invoice reduction per occurrence

7.1.3 Category III - Critical Deficiencies

Definition: Health/safety violations or regulatory non-compliance
Examples: OSHA violations, emergency response failures, contamination incidents
Response Time: Immediate correction with 24-hour root cause analysis
Documentation: Comprehensive incident report, detailed remediation plan
Financial Impact: 10-15 percent monthly invoice reduction, potential contract suspension

7.2 Corrective Action Process

7.2.1 Issue Identification and Notification

  1. Detection: Surveillance activity, customer complaint, or contractor self-report
  2. Classification: COR determines deficiency category within 4 hours
  3. Notification: Formal notice to Contractor Project Manager
  4. Acknowledgment: Contractor confirmation within 2 hours

7.2.2 Corrective Action Plan Development

  1. Root Cause Analysis: Contractor investigation and analysis
  2. Plan Submission: Detailed corrective action plan within specified timeframe
  3. COR Review: Plan evaluation and approval/modification
  4. Implementation: Contractor executes approved plan

7.2.3 Verification and Closure

  1. Verification Inspection: COR validates corrective actions
  2. Effectiveness Assessment: Follow-up monitoring for sustained improvement
  3. Closure Documentation: Formal closure with lessons learned
  4. Trend Analysis: Integration into performance evaluation system

7.3 Escalation Matrix for Critical Deficiencies

Escalation Sequence:

  1. Category III Critical Deficiency Detected
  2. COR Immediate Notification (< 2 hours)
  3. Contractor Project Manager Response (< 2 hours)
  4. 24-Hour Corrective Action Required
  5. If Unresolved → Alternate COR Activated
  6. If Still Unresolved → Contracting Officer Notification
  7. Potential Contract Suspension/Show Cause Notice
  8. If Pattern Established → Contract Termination Consideration

Notification Chain:

  1. Immediate (0-2 hours): COR → Contractor Project Manager
  2. Escalation Level 1 (2-24 hours): Alternate COR → Facilities Manager
  3. Escalation Level 2 (24-48 hours): Contracting Officer → Agency Leadership
  4. Escalation Level 3 (48+ hours): Legal Counsel → Contract Termination Review
