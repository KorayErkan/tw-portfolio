# Phase 1: QASP Document Analysis
## Quality Assurance Surveillance Plan Research

### What is a QASP?
A **Quality Assurance Surveillance Plan (QASP)** is a government-developed document used to systematically monitor contractor performance in Performance-Based Service Contracting (PBSC). It ensures that contractor quality control efforts are timely, effective, and deliver the results specified in the contract.

### Market Value
- **Hourly Rate**: $75-150/hour
- **Target Clients**: Defense contractors, federal agencies, government consulting firms
- **Document Length**: 15-50 pages
- **Complexity**: Highly structured, regulatory compliance critical

## QASP Document Structure Analysis

### Critical Components (Every QASP Must Have)

#### 1. **Performance Requirements Summary (PRS)**
- Details specific performance standards and metrics
- Corresponds directly to Performance Work Statement (PWS)
- Defines quality, quantity, and timeliness requirements
- Creates measurable objectives

#### 2. **Methods of Surveillance**
Five primary surveillance techniques:
- **Contractor Metrics**: Self-reporting by contractor
- **Random Sampling**: Statistical quality checks
- **Periodic Inspection**: Scheduled reviews
- **100% Inspection**: Complete verification (critical tasks)
- **Customer Feedback**: End-user satisfaction surveys
- **Third Party Audits**: Independent verification

#### 3. **Sampling Guide**
- Written procedures for quality checks
- Defines Acceptable Quality Level (AQL)
- Specifies what will be checked and how
- Provides accept/reject criteria

#### 4. **Decision Tables**
- Identifies unsatisfactory performance types
- Lists probable cause factors
- Defines corrective actions
- Assigns responsibility (contractor vs. government)

#### 5. **Checklists**
- Records what has been checked
- Documents information not covered by sampling
- Provides systematic verification process

### Additional Required Elements

#### 6. **Team Collaboration Framework**
- How government and contractor personnel work together
- Communication protocols and responsibilities
- Performance data handling procedures

#### 7. **Performance Improvement Process**
- Methods for identifying efficiency improvements
- Risk reduction strategies
- Continuous improvement mechanisms

#### 8. **Dispute Resolution Procedures**
- How performance disputes will be handled
- Escalation processes
- Documentation requirements

## Document Organization Pattern

### Standard QASP Structure
1. **Title Page**
2. **Table of Contents**
3. **Introduction/Purpose**
4. **Scope of Work Overview**
5. **Performance Requirements Summary**
6. **Surveillance Methods**
7. **Sampling Plans and AQL**
8. **Decision Tables**
9. **Checklists and Forms**
10. **Reporting Requirements**
11. **Performance Assessment Procedures**
12. **Appendices** (forms, templates, references)

---

## Key Writing Characteristics

### Language and Tone
- **Formal, regulatory style**
- **Precise, measurable language**
- **Clear acceptance/rejection criteria**
- **No ambiguous terms or subjective measures**

### Content Requirements
- **Specific performance metrics** (not general statements)
- **Quantifiable standards** (percentages, timeframes, quantities)
- **Clear consequences** for non-compliance
- **Detailed procedures** for all surveillance activities

### Regulatory Compliance
- Must align with Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 37.604
- Follows Performance-Based Service Contracting guidelines
- Incorporates agency-specific requirements
- Ensures legal defensibility

## Industry Context

### When QASPs are Required
- Government service contracts over certain thresholds
- Performance-based contracting arrangements
- Critical or complex service delivery
- Multi-year service agreements
- High-risk or high-value contracts

### Common Service Categories
- IT services and support
- Facilities management
- Security services
- Maintenance and repair
- Professional services
- Training and education services
