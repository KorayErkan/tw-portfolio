### Step 2: Document Analysis Process

For each QASP document, systematically examine and record:

#### A. Basic Document Information

- Agency/Organization
- Document title and date
- Total page count
- Primary service category (IT, facilities, professional services, etc.)

#### B. Critical Components Analysis

Identify how each document implements the **8 Critical QASP Components**:

1. **Performance Requirements Summary (PRS)**
   - How are performance standards defined?
   - Are metrics quantifiable?
   - What quality/quantity/timeliness measures are used?

2. **Methods of Surveillance**
   - Which of the 5 methods are used? (Contractor metrics, random sampling, periodic inspection, 100% inspection, customer feedback, third party audits)
   - How is each method described?
   - What triggers each surveillance type?

3. **Sampling Guide**
   - Is there a written sampling procedure?
   - What is the Acceptable Quality Level (AQL)?
   - How are accept/reject decisions made?

4. **Decision Tables**
   - How are performance failures categorized?
   - What probable causes are identified?
   - How is responsibility assigned?

5. **Checklists**
   - What types of checklists are provided?
   - How detailed are the verification procedures?
   - What items are covered?

6. **Team Collaboration Framework**
   - How are government/contractor roles defined?
   - What communication protocols exist?
   - How is data sharing handled?

7. **Performance Improvement Process**
   - How are improvements identified?
   - What risk reduction methods are specified?
   - Is there a continuous improvement process?

8. **Dispute Resolution Procedures**
   - How are disputes handled?
   - What escalation processes exist?
   - What documentation is required?

#### C. Document Organization Analysis

- Overall structure and flow
- Section numbering system
- Use of appendices and attachments
- Visual elements (tables, charts, diagrams)

### Step 4: Analysis Summary

Write a 2-3 paragraph summary addressing:

1. **Similarities**: What structural elements are consistent across all three documents?
2. **Variations**: How do the documents differ in approach, detail level, or organization?
3. **Effectiveness**: Which document appears most comprehensive/useful and why?
4. **Learning Points**: What insights did you gain about QASP structure and requirements?
